Mission French Africa

December 2019

Problem

Mission French Africa’s website is outdated, and needs better usability. The goal is to update and increase functionality for visitors.

Context

Mission French Africa is a non profit organization that needed an update. The website had an old and unorganized look.

Objective

I was responsible for reorganizing and updating the UI of Mission French Africa’s website. The whole process took 5 weeks.

Website Audit

Home

A lot is happening on the home page. Keeping design simple is always a great bet. I sought to decrease the clutter and highlight the important parts to the page.

Old

Section 1

Hard to read text overlay with the picture in the background

Section 2

Great visuals, but the content is not up to date. The gala was months ago

Section 3

The most engaging piece of content is the video. We need to highlight this. There is also a good job of chunking, and a visual appealling rule of thirds.

Section 4

Great call to action, but the visuals insinuate other propaganda that may not be the best.

New

I wanted to highlight the most important elements in the home page. I have a call to action to donate at the top instead of the bottom. The most engaging piece of content is the video from the old website. This explains very well what Mission French Africa is, and a user doesn’t have to go a page deep in order to discover who they are.

About

There is no need to have two pages for the about section. A better layout can offer all the information without needing to toggle between both.

Old

Who We Are:

Great chunking for vision, what we do, and community. There is a lack of visual structure to what we believe.

Staff and Board:

There are some layout issues. There is a portrat out of alignment with the others. Not all portraits are the same size. The group photo is not center aligned with the page.

New

I used more engaging imagery. Utilizing icons and a map to show the impacted areas in Africa. All of the about information is included in this one page providing an easier usability instead of toggling between three pages.

Ministries

There is no need to have three pages for the Ministries section. Again, a user will want to view all Ministries at once, and click into each for more information.

Old

Evangelism:

Difficult to read, with different sized pictures. It throws the alignment of the page off. The red text in “Stadium Evangelistic Ralleys” is center aligned when the other sections are not.

Discipleship:

This is a very long list. It has great information, but lacks an interesting flow. It is good for F-shaped eye tracking, but it lacks an interesting aesthetics.

Women’s Ministries:

Best visuals and structure for the website. I would include better h2 headlines, with the same alignment.

New

I have all three ministry areas on one page. Each section shares a sneak peak into each ministry. The user can choose to learn more about each section if interested.

Donate

The old website made my eyes bounce around, and I did not know where to focus my attention. The Donate page needs an obvious call to action with a variety of payment options and a clear process.

Old

Donate:

The call to action is very small for the page. I do like the descriptions of where the money will go.

New

I added different payment options with a selection of where you want the money to go to.

Contact

The contact page is already well put togehter, but the new one follows some basic UI design principles. I also added the address to this page to complete the contact info.

Old

Contact:

The form fields can be smaller and fit into the appropriate amount of space needed. The contact description is also too long.

New

I utilized Material Design for the form fills, and I decreased the size of the fill boxes to reflect the amount of text that will be put into each form field.
