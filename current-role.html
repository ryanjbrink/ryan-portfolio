HCA Healthcare

Staff Product Designer

Designing digital care experiences for clinicians and patients across one of the nation’s largest health systems.

Core working ethos

01 / Method

Ethnographic research

Observing real shifts, interruptions, and workarounds — uncovering the insights clinicians won’t (or can’t) put in a survey.

  • Floor time with care teams and patients
  • Context that explains the “why” behind behavior
  • Insights that humanize technology at scale

02 / Method

Service design

Mapping the full care journey — people, tools, handoffs, and friction — so product work improves the service, not just a single screen.

  • End-to-end journeys across roles and settings
  • Blueprinting where digital and physical care meet
  • Prioritizing moments that change outcomes

03 / Method

Forward-deployed design

Designing beside the people who will live in the product — embedding with partners so solutions stay honest under real constraints.

  • Co-creation with clinical and operational partners
  • Prototypes tested where the work happens
  • Decisions grounded in live feedback, not assumptions
